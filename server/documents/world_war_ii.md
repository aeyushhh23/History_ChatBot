# World War II: Global Conflict (1939–1945)

World War II was a global conflict that lasted from 1939 to 1945, involving the vast majority of the world's nations, organized into two opposing military alliances: the Allies and the Axis powers. It was the deadliest conflict in human history, resulting in an estimated 70 to 85 million fatalities, characterized by significant events such as the Holocaust, strategic bombing campaigns, and the deployment of the first atomic weapons.

The war began in Europe on September 1, 1939, when Nazi Germany, under the leadership of Adolf Hitler, invaded Poland. This prompted France and Great Britain to declare war on Germany. In June 1941, Hitler launched Operation Barbarossa, a massive invasion of the Soviet Union, bringing Joseph Stalin’s USSR into the Allied camp. Meanwhile, in Asia, Japan sought to dominate East Asia and the Pacific. On December 7, 1941, Japan launched a surprise attack on the U.S. naval base at Pearl Harbor, Hawaii. This event brought the United States, led by President Franklin D. Roosevelt, officially into the war on the Allied side.

The turning point of the war came between late 1942 and early 1943. The Soviet victory at the Battle of Stalingrad shattered the German army's offensive capabilities on the Eastern Front. In the Pacific, the U.S. Navy defeated Japanese forces at the Battle of Midway. The Allies launched the D-Day invasion of Normandy on June 6, 1944, under the command of General Dwight D. Eisenhower, establishing a Western Front in Europe. 

By early 1945, Soviet forces had advanced to Berlin from the east, while Western Allies pushed from the west. Hitler committed suicide on April 30, 1945, and Germany surrendered unconditionally on May 8, 1945, celebrated as Victory in Europe (V-E) Day. The war in the Pacific concluded after the United States dropped atomic bombs on the Japanese cities of Hiroshima and Nagasaki on August 6 and 9, 1945. Emperor Hirohito announced Japan's surrender, which was formally signed aboard the USS Missouri on September 2, 1945, ending the global conflict.
