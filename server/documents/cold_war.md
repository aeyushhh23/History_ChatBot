# The Cold War (1947–1991)

The Cold War was a period of geopolitical tension between the United States and the Soviet Union, and their respective allies, the Western Bloc (capitalist democracies) and the Eastern Bloc (communist states). It lasted from 1947, with the announcement of the Truman Doctrine, until the dissolution of the Soviet Union in 1991. The conflict was termed "cold" because there was no direct, large-scale fighting between the two superpowers, though they supported opposing sides in major regional proxy wars.

The roots of the division lay in the ideological differences and wartime mistrust between the Allies during World War II. Following the war, Europe was divided along the "Iron Curtain." In 1948, the Soviet Union blockade of Berlin led to the Berlin Airlift. In 1949, the United States and Western allies formed the North Atlantic Treaty Organization (NATO), while the Soviet Union and its satellites formed the Warsaw Pact in 1955. 

The Cold War was characterized by an intense nuclear arms race, dictated by the doctrine of Mutually Assured Destruction (MAD), and the Space Race, which culminated in the U.S. moon landing in 1969. The tension reached its absolute peak during the Cuban Missile Crisis in October 1962, when the USSR deployed nuclear missiles in Cuba, bringing the superpowers to the brink of nuclear war before a compromise was reached. Throughout the period, proxy conflicts erupted worldwide, most notably the Korean War (1950–1953) and the Vietnam War (1955–1975).

The final phase of the Cold War began in the mid-1985 when Mikhail Gorbachev became the Soviet leader. Gorbachev introduced the reforms of Glasnost (political openness) and Perestroika (economic restructuring) to revitalize the stagnant Soviet state. These reforms unintentionally weakened communist control over Eastern Europe. In November 1989, the Berlin Wall was opened, leading to the reunification of Germany. The Cold War officially ended in December 1991 when the Soviet Union dissolved, resulting in the independence of 15 new republics.
